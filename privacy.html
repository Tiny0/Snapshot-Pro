Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Overview

Snapshot Pro is built as a native macOS application. We value your privacy and believe in transparency. This policy explains how your data is handled within the app.

2. Data Collection

Snapshot Pro does not collect or store any of your personal data on our servers. We do not track your usage, collect your screenshots, or monitor your activity.

3. Local Storage

All projects, scenes, and image layers you create are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data remains on your computer unless you choose to sync it via iCloud or delete the application support files.

4. iCloud Synchronization

If you enable iCloud functionality for Snapshot Pro, your project data will be synchronized across your personal Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This data is stored in your private iCloud database, and we (the developers) have no access to this information.

5. Third-Party Services

Snapshot Pro does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The application operates entirely offline, except for the optional iCloud synchronization mentioned above.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our GitHub repository or App Store contact information.

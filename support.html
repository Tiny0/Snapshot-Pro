Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I export screenshots without an alpha channel?

A: When you click the export button, Snapshot Pro will prompt you with export settings. Simply uncheck the "Include Alpha Channel" box to ensure your images comply with strict App Store Connect requirements.

Q: Will I lose my project data if I uninstall the app?

A: Snapshot Pro stores your data locally using SwiftData. Standard app deletion will leave your application support databases intact. However, if you use a dedicated third-party app uninstaller, it might wipe out local databases. We highly recommend utilizing iCloud sync to ensure data is safe across reinstalls.

Q: How do I create a panoramic image flow?

A: In the Screenshot Control panel, increase the "Screen Count". Then, place a wide panoramic screenshot or span your images across the canvas offsets. The editor automatically slices it perfectly into single screen panels upon export.
